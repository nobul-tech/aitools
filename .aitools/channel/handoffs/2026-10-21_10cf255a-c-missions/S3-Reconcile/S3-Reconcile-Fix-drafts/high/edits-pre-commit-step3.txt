FX-2a (high): check-pre-commit step 3 with --fix / -Fix runs build-deploy.sh
with no exit check (sh: `>/dev/null 2>&1` under set -e aborts the checklist on a
failed build; ps1: `2>$null | Out-Null`, result discarded), which deletes and
rebuilds the REAL dotprofile <userRepoPath>/deploy/ from the branch being
committed, then `git add deploy/` stages the frozen aitools/deploy/ and reports
PASS "(rebuilt + staged)". Fix (plan Q11, suggested answer): step 3 never
rebuilds; it WARNs and names pre-push step 7 (temporary dotprofile copy, every
push) and the post-merge rebuild. Base: origin/main (no open PR touches either
file). Step 10 is left as is: it now checks only that the frozen
aitools/deploy/ has no unstaged changes (the R12a D-15 text says exactly that).
