## Why it was held

The approved text says rules reach the agent tools "through the
`setup-user-claude` / `setup-user-cursor` fan-out". On origin/main 8a9dcb5 only
`setup-user-cursor` fans out `~/.aitools/rules` (`scripts/setup-user-cursor.sh`
line 389: `HARNESS_RULES_DIR="$HOME/.aitools/rules"`). `setup-user-claude`
still deploys rules from the dotprofile (`scripts/setup-user-claude.sh` header,
line 13: `# Managed: ~/.claude/rules/*.md matching <userRepoPath>/claude/rules/`);
`grep -E "aitools[/\\]rules"` on `setup-user-claude.sh` / `.ps1` finds nothing.
The approved deploy-paths.md text says the same ("`setup-user-claude` once it
fans out").
