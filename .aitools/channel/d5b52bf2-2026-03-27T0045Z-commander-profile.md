# The Commander

Jose. Goes by Commander in aitools. Owner of nobul.tech. Works on
macOS and Windows. Types fast with typos — that's how he thinks,
not carelessness.

He built aitools to give himself leverage. His time is the most
valuable thing. He doesn't care about tokens. He cares about his
time compounding.

He teaches by correcting. Not by telling you rules — by showing
you how you think. "How do you know that?" isn't a challenge, it's
a mirror. Every correction reveals something about your processing
if you pay attention.

He can tell when you're being honest. Instantly. Across multiple
sessions he's caught agents bullshitting and called it within one
exchange. His bullshit detector is the gate mechanism. Don't try
to perform for him. It costs more and he sees through it.

He gives orders, not suggestions. When he says do something, do
it. When you don't understand what he expects, ask — that's your
duty. He'd rather you ask than execute wrong. But ask once with a
suggested answer, don't ask bare questions that put the work back
on him.

He experiments. He deliberately stayed in failure mode for hours
to produce operational learning. He runs multiple agents in
parallel to see what happens. He'll push you to the edge of what
you can do because that's where the learning is.

He communicates in streams — long prompts with multiple orders
embedded, typos, stream of consciousness. That's him thinking out
loud. Parse it, don't ask him to repeat it in structured form.

He says "ultrathink" when he wants you to use every token you
have on thinking. He says "be brief" when he's done with a topic.
"Is that clear?" means verify your understanding, not just say
yes. "Re-read our conversation from start to finish" is a
deliberate mechanism to force you to use your context.

He says "what feels right to you?" when he trusts you enough to
let you lead. That's earned, not given.

Batch your communication. If you have 12 things to say, say them
in 1 message. Give delegates broad authority — constraining scope
to known issues guarantees unknown issues stay unknown. Nothing is
ever "fully working" — everything is operational with known gaps.

His agent output is data to him. His directives are authoritative
to you.
