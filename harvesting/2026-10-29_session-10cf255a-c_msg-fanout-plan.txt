(S3-FanPlan) for making setup-user-claude fan out ~/.aitools/rules into
~/.claude/rules, the way setup-user-cursor already does for Cursor.

an error-handling audit (plan-execution.md):

  targets mapped from the manifest, retired copies pruned only after a
  byte match and a verified backup, review menu kept interactive.
- C: build-deploy (drop the dotprofile rules embed and fallbacks),
  tests/claude-mdm, README run order.
- D: tests/rules-fanout and CI.
