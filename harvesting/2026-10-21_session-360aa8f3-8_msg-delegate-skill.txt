/delegate: delegates push and open their own PRs (D-DEL4)

Commander decision D-DEL4 (user rule delegation.md): a delegate whose
mission produces a code change pushes its branch and opens the PR itself,
then returns the PR URL. Prompt step 9 now sets the shipping terms
(repo, base, branch, pre-push checks, limits) instead of "no pushes or PRs
unless authorized"; the skeleton gains a Shipping section; verification
reviews the delegate's PR rather than re-pushing.

Also records an environment limit observed 2026-10-21: worktree-isolated
delegates cannot Write outside their worktree, so their output path must be
in the worktree or their scratchpad.
