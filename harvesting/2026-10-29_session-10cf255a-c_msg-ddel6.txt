Add D-DEL6: conflict priority for delegates

Decision D-DEL6 (commander, 2026-10-26): a delegate resolves conflicting
instructions in this order: 1) the commander's own words, newest first
(recent session transcripts, and the rules, standing orders and decision
records that record his words); 2) the delegation prompt; 3) skills. A
prompt cannot loosen a rule unless it quotes the commander. A mandated
skill or rule is never dropped silently.

Inbound duty 7 (new): follow the non-conflicting parts of whatever lost and
report each `CONFLICT: <A> vs <B> -- resolved by <tier>: <what was done>`;
the delegating agent pre-resolves foreseeable skill conflicts in the
prompt (aitools /delegate skill writing step 12).

Why: in mission MDM-WINTOOL a delegate silently skipped the mandated
deep-research skill because its file locations clashed with the prompt
(aitools incident #171, main 6686c16).
