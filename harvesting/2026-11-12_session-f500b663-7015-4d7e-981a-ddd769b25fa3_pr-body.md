- **Migration:** the manifest planner treats `aitools` and `dotprofile` as *withdrawn sources*. A previous manifest line with either source is retired on the next run when no source has the name, even though neither source is listed and whether or not a dotprofile is configured. Without this, everything already deployed from those sources would be kept forever ("source not listed") and keep reaching `~/.claude` and `~/.cursor`.
