## How to use this issue

This issue is the specification for the implementation. Whoever implements it:
1. **Reads** this body in full, and its comments: the commander's answers of 2026-11-15, and the March history.
2. **Writes the RFC** (section 7, WP-0). It carries the decisions of section 1 as normative text and settles every point marked "decide in the RFC" in section 6.
3. **Writes the plan.** Work packages WP-1 to WP-6, in batches of two or three files, each with its verification.
4. **Implements under D-DEL4:** its own worktree, its own PR, tests on macOS, Linux and Windows, and new hook checks in observe mode first.

The decisions in section 1 are the commander's; the RFC does not reopen them. A point the RFC finds unclear goes to the commander as an RFI with a suggested answer.
